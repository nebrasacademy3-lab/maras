# Email icon assets

PNG renditions of the Font Awesome 6 Free brand glyphs already installed through
the mobile app's @expo/vector-icons dependency. Rendered on white in the site's
primary blue #1258e8, 96 × 96, displayed at 36 × 36 in email. Social trademarks
remain the property of their respective owners; these icons label account links
and do not imply sponsorship.

Source: https://fontawesome.com/
License: https://fontawesome.com/license/free (icons CC BY 4.0; fonts SIL OFL 1.1).
Rebuild with scripts/generate-email-assets.mjs using the local Playwright runtime.
No font file is published in this directory.
